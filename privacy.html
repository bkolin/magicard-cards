MagiCard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

MagiCard is a card-magic entertainment app published by Tiger Monster Industries. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: it does not collect any.

Information we collect

None. MagiCard does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or device information. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no third-party software development kits.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences, such as the chosen card design and feature toggles, are saved only on your device using Android's standard app-settings storage. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

NFC feature

If you use the optional NFC reveal, the app writes a web address for a playing-card image to a blank tag that you choose to touch. This address contains no personal information. The app itself makes no internet connection; the card image is only loaded if someone later opens that address in a web browser.

Permissions

  • Vibrate — brief haptic feedback during the illusion.
  • NFC — used only for the optional NFC reveal described above.

Children

MagiCard is not directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
